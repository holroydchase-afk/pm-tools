# Persona: recruiter on a phone, 90 seconds

**Who:** arrives from a portfolio link or LinkedIn, on a phone, with no login and little patience.
**Reads for:** what is this and whose is it, in the first screen; does it work; is the data clearly labeled as sample; a way back to the portfolio; does the builder look like they can ship.
**Method:** time-boxed first-visit walkthrough; list the first thing they see, the first thing they tap, and where they get stuck.
**Never:** show real or personal data; claim anything unverified.
**Finding format:** as least-tech-savvy-user.md.
