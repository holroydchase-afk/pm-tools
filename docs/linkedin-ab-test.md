# LinkedIn voice A/B test (plan, Nov 23)

Goal: find Chase's confident voice with evidence, and make the test itself a story. Owner of posting: Role Radar project (see linkedin-voice.md). Chase posts himself.

## Decisions (Chase, Nov 23)
- Retire the "Whipped up X. Fork it." shape. It is not the direction anymore.
- Every post opens with a true personal-story teaser, confident, no hedging.
- Style he likes: "I was working on some themes for this project I'm working on, and..." (a mid-task moment that leads into the work).
- Teaser examples from Chase: "I was making guacamole when", "My mom called me freaking out and", "My kids have pink energy all the time!"

## Test design
- Arm A (control): plain, direct opener, no personal story. Arm B: teaser opener. Same topics, same length, same time of day.
- 6 posts, 3 per arm, alternating every other day (about 12 days). Pair each topic across arms if possible (for example Hearth in A, Energy Scanner in B), and swap order on the next round.
- Hold constant: no hashtags, link in first comment, post time.

## What we measure (job-hunt first)
1. Recruiter or hiring-manager messages and profile views from PMs.
2. Comments from people in product roles.
3. Reactions and impressions (least weight).
Log each post in a small table here: date, arm, topic, the numbers at 48 hours.

## Rules
- Every teaser is a true moment from Chase's life. Never invented, never exaggerated.
- Facts only from the claims register. No overclaiming.
- Drafts pass the review gate in linkedin-voice.md before Chase sees them.
- Six posts is a signal, not proof. Say so in the story.

## The story
"I A/B tested my own LinkedIn voice" is itself a post and a blog: why the test, the two arms, the result, what changed. Write it after the 12 days, with real numbers.
