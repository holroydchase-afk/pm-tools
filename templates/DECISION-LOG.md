# Decision log

_Every decision with why and what it was chosen over, so anyone can retell the story ("we chose X because…"). Add an entry in the same PR that acts on the call. Product decisions also need a persona check in `decisions/` (see `PERSONA-CHECK.md`)._

## DEC-1: <one line>
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD> · **Decided by:** <owner, quoted> / proposed
- **Chose:** <option>
- **Over:** <the alternatives>
- **Why:** <the reason, with a source>
- **Undo cost:** <cheap / a migration / irreversible>
- **Persona check:** `decisions/<file>.md`
